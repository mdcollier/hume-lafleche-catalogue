La Flèche study

The La Flèche library catalogue and Hume: comparisons and zones

Books recorded in the catalogue of the library of the Jesuit college at La Flèche, where Hume lived from 1735 to 1737, are compared passage by passage with Hume's texts.

These pages record textual relations that may warrant historical investigation. They are not evidence that Hume read, used, borrowed from, recalled or replied to any of these books.
