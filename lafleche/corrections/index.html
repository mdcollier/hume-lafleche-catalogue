Claim ceiling. This site presents approved source previews, catalogue materials, computational routing metadata, and review records to support scholarly inspection. It does not claim that Hume read, used, borrowed from, contacted, depended on, or was influenced by any listed text.

These pages record textual relations that may warrant historical investigation. They are not evidence that Hume read, used, borrowed from, recalled or replied to any of these books.

This page lists what the project corrected, withdrew, superseded or stopped. It is kept because a record of errors is part of the record.

In these records, 'contact' and 'contact zone' are the project's working terms for a textual relation that its method registers between passages. They do not mean that Hume met, read or used the book.

Paths such as artifacts/la_fleche_wave_2/WAVE2_FIRST_PASS_DESCRIPTIVE_REPORT__CORRECTION_1.md are provenance identifiers for the project's internal records; they are not links and the files are not public.

  • Trace run locating where fifteen known pairs were lost result: negative status: corrected A qualification of 25 September 2026 reclassified the run as a diagnostic of the machinery that proposes candidate pairs: 1 of the 15 pairs was excluded by close reading and 10 of the remaining 14 were labelled as not worth a scholar's reading (docs/RESEARCH_LEDGER.md L-13, R3 relation-pool entry, qualification).

Open questions in the project's records

Inconsistencies and unresolved questions found in the project's own records. They are listed so that a reader can weigh the studies concerned; each is repaired when the work next touches it, and none has been settled by guessing.
