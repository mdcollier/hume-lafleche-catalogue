This catalogue documents the recorded historical holdings of the library of the former Jesuit college at La Flèche. It is not a record of what Hume read or used, not a Hume reading list, and not the British Contexts comparison track. Record states are project routing/review metadata, not claims of Humean influence, contact, source-dependence, reading, or coverage.

The catalogue of the library of the former Jesuit college (the Collège royal) of La Flèche, compiled by the bookseller Riboutet in December 1776 – January 1777. The catalogue of the library on which these pages rest was compiled in December 1776 and January 1777, some forty years after Hume lived in the town of La Flèche (1735–1737), and after the Jesuits had ceased to run the college. A book listed in it was held by the library at that later date; the catalogue does not by itself show that the book, or that edition, was there in Hume's time.

Source: Projet La Flèche, Université du Québec à Montréal — 'À propos de l'édition' (https://projetlafleche.uqam.ca/a-propos-edition) and 'Histoire de la bibliothèque' (https://projetlafleche.uqam.ca/histoire-de-la-bibliotheque).

The catalogue records, their identifications and their transcription are those of the edition of the catalogue by Projet La Flèche, Université du Québec à Montréal (https://projetlafleche.uqam.ca/). The project took the records from the edition in March 2026, and they carry the edition's identifiers.

What is counted. A record is one bibliographic identification in the UQAM edition; one numbered entry of the manuscript catalogue can yield several records (several copies, works bound together, or identifications the edition marks as uncertain). The records taken in March 2026 number 2,493, under 2,029 numbered entries, and carry 2,490 distinct identifiers, because three identifiers occur twice. When the records were taken, the edition stated 2,692 notices, with two manuscript pages not yet transcribed. The 22 section labels are given as transcribed, including evident spelling variants; the manuscript's own sectioning may have fewer sections.
