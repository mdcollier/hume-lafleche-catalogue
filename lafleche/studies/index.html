Claim ceiling. This site presents approved source previews, catalogue materials, computational routing metadata, and review records to support scholarly inspection. It does not claim that Hume read, used, borrowed from, contacted, depended on, or was influenced by any listed text.

These pages record textual relations that may warrant historical investigation. They are not evidence that Hume read, used, borrowed from, recalled or replied to any of these books.

Terms: see the glossary

Results are given as the project's records state them: positive, negative, inconclusive, mixed, not completed, or descriptive. Negative and uncompleted studies are listed with the rest.

In these records, 'contact' and 'contact zone' are the project's working terms for a textual relation that its method registers between passages. They do not mean that Hume met, read or used the book.

78 studies are listed, grouped by kind. Each has a page that gives the question, what was done, the result and any correction, as a summary written by a model from the project's records. What was corrected, withdrawn, superseded or stopped is also gathered on the corrections page.

Each result describes the outcome of the study's own question. A positive result for a question about text preparation, retrieval or method is not evidence that Hume was influenced by any book. The status describes the record of a study, the result its outcome: a study can be 'not completed' while its record is 'current'.

A synthesis of the lessons recorded in the studies, followed by the lessons themselves, is on the method page.
