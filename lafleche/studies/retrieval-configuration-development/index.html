The question

Which retrieval unit and combination of channels best recovers documented parallels across French and English?

What was done

Window sizes, sentence-bounded spans, three retrieval channels and their fusion were varied one at a time on two development texts with documented parallels to Hume, in blinded comparisons. The best configuration was frozen as the reference baseline for all later texts.

Result

The record's wording is that the hybrid configuration 'is the best tested cross-lingual configuration' (provisional) and that 'no single configuration recovers all three documented Perinetti parallels'. It also records that eight single-variable retrieval experiments found no improvement. Provisional findings add that the two lexical channels are 'largely non-functional cross-lingually'.

Lessons

  • Retrieve narrow, interpret wide: the retrieval unit and the scoring unit should differ.
  • Hub passages dominate more in cross-lingual than in same-language retrieval.
