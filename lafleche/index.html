Claim ceiling. This site presents approved source previews, catalogue materials, computational routing metadata, and review records to support scholarly inspection. It does not claim that Hume read, used, borrowed from, contacted, depended on, or was influenced by any listed text.

Books recorded in the catalogue of the library of the Jesuit college at La Flèche, where Hume lived from 1735 to 1737, are compared passage by passage with Hume's texts. Each comparison pairs one or more paragraphs of a French book with one or more paragraphs of Hume, and a zone is a region of repeated or localised convergence between a book and a section of Hume.

These pages record textual relations that may warrant historical investigation. They are not evidence that Hume read, used, borrowed from, recalled or replied to any of these books.

About the catalogue

This project reconstructs the Collège de La Flèche / Riboulet library catalogue as a scholarly working environment for studying the intellectual setting associated with Hume's residence at La Flèche in the 1730s. It combines catalogue orientation, approved source previews, method records, and review-workflow materials.

The public materials are designed for orientation and scholarly inspection; they do not establish what Hume read, used, or drew upon.

This project documents the recorded historical holdings of the Collège de La Flèche / Riboulet library. It is not a record of what Hume read or used, and not the British Contexts comparison track. Record states are project routing/review metadata, not claims of Humean influence, contact, source-dependence, reading, or coverage.
